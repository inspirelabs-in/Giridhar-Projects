Product Principles

Principle 01
Earn in 3 Taps

Every earning action should complete in 3 taps or fewer. Copy product link, paste in GrabCash, share affiliate link. If a workflow takes more than 3 taps, we need to simplify it. This applies to link creation, deal sharing, and withdrawal flows.

Principle 02
Show the Money

Users must see their earnings update in real-time. Every click tracked, every conversion visible, every rupee accounted for. The dashboard should feel like watching your bank balance grow. Transparency builds trust and drives engagement.

Principle 03
Mobile-First, Always

85% of our users are on mobile. Every feature, every flow, every interaction must be designed for a 6-inch screen held in one hand. Desktop is secondary. The mobile app is the primary platform, not an afterthought.

Principle 04
Vernacular by Default

India's next 100M affiliate earners don't speak English as their primary language. Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali support is not a feature but a requirement. Content, UI, and notifications must speak the user's language.

Principle 05
Data-Informed Decisions

Every product decision backed by data. A/B test everything. Measure impact within 7 days. Kill features that don't move the needle within 30 days. No vanity metrics; only actionable insights that drive earnings growth for users.

Principle 06
Community Over Competition

Foster a community of earners who help each other succeed. Sharing tips, best deals, and strategies. Top earners mentor newcomers. Competition exists, but the pie grows for everyone. Network effects create a moat no competitor can replicate.

Platform Limitations

Scale Ceiling

Current architecture supports approximately 10,000 concurrent users. Beyond this, Vercel serverless cold starts and Supabase connection pooling become bottlenecks. Need to migrate to dedicated infrastructure by 226K registered users.

Single Point of Failure

Telegram bot runs on a single server instance with no redundancy. An outage in the bot takes down the primary earning channel for 40% of active users. Need multi-region deployment and auto-failover.

Affiliate Network Dependency

Commission tracking relies on third-party network pixel fires and postback URLs. Average attribution delay is 24-48 hours. Some networks have 72-hour delays. No server-side tracking fallback exists.

No Offline Capability

Web app requires constant internet connection. No service worker, no offline caching, no background sync. Users on patchy 2G/3G connections in tier-2/3 cities face broken experiences.

Data Analytics Gap

No data warehouse or analytics pipeline. Business insights are derived from raw SQL queries on the production database. No event tracking beyond basic page views. Attribution and funnel analysis impossible.

Payment Infrastructure

UPI payouts processed manually via Razorpay dashboard. No automated payout scheduling, no bulk processing, no reconciliation system. Operations team manually approves each withdrawal request above ₹1,000.

AI/ML Product Features

Machine learning is the core differentiator. From deal recommendations to fraud detection, AI maximizes earnings for users and revenue for the platform.

Deal Recommendation Engine
  • Model: Two-tower neural network for candidate retrieval + XGBoost ranker
  • User Features: Category history, click patterns, earning amounts, time of day, device
  • Deal Features: Commission rate, discount %, brand popularity, category, price bucket
  • Context Features: Day of week, upcoming sales, trending categories, user location
  • Training: Daily retrain on 14-day rolling window, ~5M interaction events
  • Serving: FAISS index for ANN retrieval (sub-10ms), XGBoost ranking (sub-50ms)
  • Target: 15% CTR on recommended deals (vs 3% baseline on chronological feed)
Price Prediction Model
  • Purpose: Predict optimal time to share a deal (when price is lowest)
  • Model: LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) on historical price time series
  • Features: Historical prices, day of week, sale calendar, competitor prices
  • Output: Price forecast for next 7 days with confidence interval
  • Use Case: "Price likely to drop 15% during upcoming sale in 3 days"
  • Data Source: Internal price scraping pipeline (hourly for top 10K products)
  • Accuracy Target: MAPE under 8% for 3-day forecasts
Churn Prediction Model
  • Purpose: Identify users likely to churn before they leave; trigger re-engagement
  • Model: Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM) with 30 features
  • Key Features: Days since last login, earning trend (increasing/decreasing), link creation velocity, withdrawal history, referral activity, notification open rate
  • Output: Churn probability score (0-1) computed daily for all users
  • Action Triggers: Score above 0.7 → personalized re-engagement campaign
  • Interventions: Bonus deal, higher commission for 48h, personalized push, email
  • Target: Reduce 30-day churn from 72% to 55% by Month 9
Fraud Detection Pipeline
  • Purpose: Detect and prevent click fraud, fake referrals, and commission manipulation
  • Model: Isolation Forest for anomaly detection + rule-based filters
  • Signals: Click velocity anomalies, IP clustering, device fingerprint patterns, conversion rate spikes, referral chain analysis
  • Rules: Max 100 clicks/link/hour, min 3-second dwell time, unique IP per click
  • Actions: Auto-flag (low confidence), auto-block (high confidence), manual review queue
  • False Positive Target: Under 2% of flagged transactions are legitimate
  • Estimated Fraud Prevention: ₹5-8L/month in prevented fraudulent payouts at scale

5 Strategic Bets That Define This Roadmap

Bet 1: Mobile-First Wins India

85% of India's internet users are mobile-only. Shipping a native app with offline support, UPI integration, and vernacular support is not optional but existential. The mobile app will be the primary revenue driver by Month 12.

Bet 2: Creator Storefronts Create Lock-in

When users build a storefront, customize it, grow followers, and share it on social profiles, switching costs become prohibitive. No competitor offers this. Storefronts transform users from commodity link-sharers into brand-builders with identity tied to GrabCash.

Bet 3: AI Is the Earnings Multiplier

Users cannot manually discover the best deals across 91 retailers (32 active). AI recommendations that surface high-converting, high-commission deals personalized to each user's audience will be the single biggest driver of earnings-per-user growth.

Bet 4: Telegram Is the Distribution Channel

India's affiliate deal-sharing culture lives on Telegram. A best-in-class Telegram bot with inline mode, channel automation, and group tools captures this market before competitors recognize the opportunity. Telegram is GrabCash's unique distribution moat.

Bet 5: Brand Portal Creates B2B Revenue Stream

Consumer SaaS (Pro subscriptions) alone cannot sustain the business at scale. The brand self-serve portal creates a B2B revenue stream where brands pay for performance marketing through GrabCash's creator network. This dual-revenue model (consumer + brand) creates a sustainable business with multiple growth levers. At 100K+ users, the creator network becomes valuable enough for brands to pay premium CPAs, creating a flywheel: more brands → higher commissions → more creators → more brands.
