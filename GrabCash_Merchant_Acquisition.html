★ Exclusive Deal Creation for Telegram Channels

Exclusive deals are the #1 retention tool. When a merchant creates a deal that's ONLY available through GrabCash Telegram channels, both the admin and the subscriber feel special.

Exclusive Deal Types

Type 1: Exclusive Coupon Code
Merchant creates a unique coupon code (e.g., GRABCASH20) that works only when accessed via GrabCash links. Extra 5-10% off on top of existing offers. Admin promotes as "exclusive deal only for our channel members."
Type 2: Early Access
GrabCash admins get 6-12 hour early access to sale events before they go public. E.g., Myntra End of Reason Sale links go live on GrabCash channels at 9 PM the night before public launch.
Type 3: Flash Deal (Limited Qty)
Merchant provides limited-quantity deals (e.g., "First 100 buyers get extra INR 200 off"). Creates urgency and FOMO. Ideal for electronics and fashion launches.
Type 4: Bundle Exclusive
Merchant creates product bundles available only via GrabCash. E.g., "Nykaa GrabCash Bundle: Lip Kit + Eye Palette at INR 999 (worth INR 1,499)." Higher AOV, exclusive feel.
Target: 50 Exclusive Deals/Month by Month 6
Each exclusive deal should generate 500+ orders minimum. At 50 deals/month, that's 25,000+ exclusive-deal orders. This becomes a powerful retention argument for both merchants ("your deals drive real volume") and admins ("exclusive deals your audience can't get elsewhere").
