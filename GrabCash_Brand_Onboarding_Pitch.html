CPA Model Advantages for Brands vs CPM/CPC

The fundamental selling point for affiliate/cashback platforms is the CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) model. Unlike CPM (Cost Per Mille) or CPC (Cost Per Click) advertising where brands pay for impressions or clicks regardless of outcome, affiliate marketing charges only when a purchase is completed. This radically shifts the risk equation.

How to Find a Brand's Current Affiliate Partners

Check CashKaro.com

2

Check EarnKaro.com

EarnKaro shows commission rates more transparently than CashKaro. Check both the commission rate and whether the brand has "exclusive" or "featured" status. 4M+ registered users on EarnKaro means significant deal volume.

3

Check CueLinks Dashboard

CueLinks lists 500+ merchants. If a brand appears, it means they're on at least one affiliate network. CueLinks shows the CPS (Cost Per Sale) percentage for each merchant.

4

Check Admitad India

Admitad lists commission details for its 600+ Indian programs. Create a free publisher account to access the full catalog. Note whether the brand offers baseline or elevated commissions.

5

Google Search: "[Brand] affiliate program India"

Many brands run their own affiliate programs through Shopify, Impact.com, or custom setups. A Google search often reveals application pages, commission details, and even case studies from existing affiliates.

Response: "Totally respect that. Here's an interesting data point: 68% of CMOs in India report increasing pressure to demonstrate revenue attribution from marketing spend. GrabCash doesn't replace brand marketing -- it complements it. Brand campaigns create demand; GrabCash captures that demand at the point of purchase. Response: "Excellent question -- this is something we take extremely seriously. GrabCash uses ML-based fraud detection that monitors for: (1) unusual purchase patterns, (2) high return rates from specific sources, (3) cookie stuffing and click injection, (4) device fingerprint anomalies. We validate all transactions during a configurable hold period (typically 30-60 days for returns). Any fraudulent transactions are automatically excluded from commission calculations. We also carry the fraud risk -- if we pay out to an admin and the order is later returned, that's our loss, not yours. For our fraud prevention framework, see Doc 12: Admin Legal, Tax & Compliance Guide."

If your gross margin on a product is 25% and you set an affiliate commission at 5%, your effective margin after commission is still 20% -- but that's on a sale you wouldn't have made otherwise. Compare this to Google Ads where you might spend INR 200 per click with a 2% conversion rate, meaning INR 10,000 CPA regardless of whether the customer stays. With GrabCash, a 5% commission on a INR 2,000 order is just INR 100 -- and you only pay on confirmed, non-returned sales."

Negotiation Tactics

Tactic 1: Anchor High, Concede Strategically

Always open with a rate at the upper end of the category range. This anchors the conversation and gives room to negotiate. For fashion, open at 15% and be prepared to land at 10-12%. Never lead with the minimum viable rate -- there's no room to maneuver. Concession sequence: Offer to reduce rate in exchange for (1) longer contract term, (2) exclusive positioning, (3) faster payment terms, or (4) access to product feed API.

Tactic 2: Tiered Performance Pricing

Propose a tiered model: base commission + performance bonus. Example: 8% base commission, escalating to 10% if GrabCash drives 1,000+ orders/month, and 12% if 2,500+ orders/month. This aligns incentives -- the brand gets a lower starting rate, and GrabCash is motivated to drive volume. Most brands find this attractive because it ties cost directly to scale.

Tactic 3: Competitive Leverage

Use competitor rates as leverage. "Your competitor [X] is already on GrabCash at [rate]%. If you match or beat that, we can offer priority positioning in our deal channels." This creates urgency and provides a market reference point. Note: Never share actual competitor rates -- use ranges and anonymized references.

Tactic 4: Pilot-to-Scale Ramp

For brands resistant to a high commission, propose: "Let's start with a 30-day pilot at [lower rate]. If the ROAS exceeds [threshold], we move to [higher rate] for the annual partnership." This reduces perceived risk and gives the brand a controlled way to test the channel. Most pilots convert to annual partnerships at higher rates because the data proves the value.

DPDP Act Compliance for Brand Partnerships

Critical: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

All GrabCash brand partnerships must comply with India's DPDP Act 2023. Key requirements:

1. Data Processing Agreement: Every brand partnership agreement must include a data processing addendum defining what data is collected, how it's used, retention periods, and deletion procedures.
2. Consent: User consent must be obtained before tracking across brand websites. GrabCash's tracking pixel must include appropriate consent mechanisms.
3. Data Minimization: Only collect data necessary for tracking and attribution. No collection of sensitive personal data.
4. Cross-Border: If brand data is processed outside India, additional safeguards required.
5. Breach Notification: Both parties must notify each other of data breaches within 72 hours.
