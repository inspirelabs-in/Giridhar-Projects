Mission Brief

▼
Strategic Priority: Telegram is where 85% of affiliate commerce in India happens. Controlling the admin network means controlling the cashback distribution layer. This is not optional — it is existential for GrabCash.

Objective

Recruit 500 active Telegram channel admins from competitor platforms (primarily EarnKaro, ExtraPe/PaisaWaapas, and CueLinks) within 180 days of program launch. These admins will become the primary distribution channel for GrabCash affiliate links, driving GMV through their combined subscriber base of 50M+ users.

Why This Matters

Telegram has become the dominant channel for affiliate commerce in India. The ecosystem works as follows:

  • Admins run Telegram channels with 10K to 1M+ subscribers who are trained to buy through affiliate links
  • Affiliate platforms (EarnKaro, ExtraPe) provide link conversion tools, deal feeds, and commission tracking
  • Merchants pay commissions on sales driven through these channels
  • An estimated 85% of EarnKaro's traffic comes through Telegram channels, making admins their most critical distribution asset
  • The top 100 Telegram deal channels collectively reach 30M+ subscribers with daily deal posts

EarnKaro's Lock-In Mechanisms (What We Must Break)

  1. EK Affiliaters Tool: Bot token integration that auto-converts links and auto-posts deals. Admins invest hours setting this up — high switching cost.

Commission competition Strategy

▼
Core Principle: "Pay More, Pay Faster, Pay Better." In the affiliate world, economics always wins. The platform that puts the most money in an admin's pocket, the fastest, with the least friction — that platform wins.

Commission Premium Tiers

GrabCash will offer structurally higher commissions than EarnKaro across all major categories. The premium strategy operates in three layers:

Layer 1: Blanket Premium (First 90 Days)

Every admin who joins GrabCash receives +20% above EarnKaro rates on ALL categories for the first 90 days. This is the headline number that gets admins interested.

Example: If EarnKaro pays 8% on Ajio, GrabCash pays 9.6% for the first 90 days. After 90 days, the rate settles to GrabCash standard (which is still higher than EK on most categories due to GrabOn's merchant relationships).

Layer 2: Top 50 Merchant Guarantee

For the top 50 merchants by transaction volume, GrabCash guarantees:

  • Rate match: If EarnKaro has a higher rate on any specific merchant, we match it
  • +5% bonus: On top of the matched rate, we add a 5% bonus
  • Real-time rate monitoring: Automated system checks EarnKaro rates daily and adjusts

Pending Commission Bridge Program

The Bridge: Solving the #1 Switching Barrier

The biggest reason admins don't switch platforms is pending commissions. An admin with ₹5L pending at EarnKaro is essentially locked in for 2-3 months. The Pending Commission Bridge program eliminates this barrier:

How It Works
  1. Admin provides screenshot of their EarnKaro dashboard showing pending commissions
  2. GrabCash team verifies the screenshot (cross-reference with channel size and posting history)
  3. GrabCash advances 50% of the verified pending amount within 48 hours via UPI/bank transfer
  4. Admin continues posting on both platforms during transition
  5. When EarnKaro commissions are confirmed and paid, admin repays the bridge from those funds (honor system + contractual)
  6. If commissions are lower than expected, GrabCash absorbs the difference up to 20%

The Problem

Top Telegram admins (Tier 1-2) often have their own accounts with affiliate networks (vCommission, Cuelinks, Admitad, etc.) and earn commissions directly. They won't want to give these up for GrabCash because:

  • They've built direct relationships with network account managers
  • They negotiate custom commission rates based on their volume
  • They fear losing control if they depend on a single platform
  • Their existing pending commissions are tied to these network accounts

Solution Framework: Two-Track Approach

Track A: GrabCash as Aggregator (Default for Tier 3-5)

For most admins, GrabCash provides pre-converted links. The admin doesn't need any network accounts:

  • Admin posts deals using GrabCash links (gc.in/xxxxx)
  • GrabCash handles all tracking, attribution, and commission collection from merchants/networks
  • Admin sees commissions in GrabCash dashboard
  • GrabCash pays admin their commission share
  • Revenue share model: Admin gets 70-85% of the commission; GrabCash retains 15-30% as platform fee
  • Higher revenue share tiers for higher volume (see table below)

Advantage for admin: Zero setup complexity, access to 4,000+ merchants without individual network accounts, single dashboard for all earnings.

Track B: Admin's Own Network Accounts (For Tier 1-2)

For top admins who insist on using their own network accounts, GrabCash facilitates rather than replaces:

  • GrabCash helps admins get approved for premium network accounts through GrabOn's relationships
  • Admins use their own tracking links but benefit from GrabCash Bot tools (auto-forward, analytics, scheduling)
  • GrabCash provides deal feed but admin converts links through their own accounts
  • Platform fee model: Admin pays a small monthly platform fee (₹999-4,999) for GrabCash Bot tools instead of revenue share
  • GrabCash still provides value: deal sourcing, analytics, community, growth support

Advantage for admin: Keep 100% of commissions, get better network deals through GrabOn's relationships, access superior tools.

Admin Persona Deep Profiles

To help recruiters understand who they're talking to, here are detailed personas representing the most common admin archetypes encountered in the Telegram deal ecosystem:

Persona A: "The Full-Time Professional" (Tier 2-3)
  • Demographics: Male, 25-35 years old, based in tier-2 city (Lucknow, Jaipur, Indore), started as college side-hustle
  • Channel: 1-2 channels with 80K-300K combined subscribers, multi-category deals with focus on fashion and electronics
  • Daily routine: Wakes at 8 AM, checks deal feeds by 9 AM, posts 30-50 deals per day, monitors earnings at night
  • Earnings: ₹80K-2.5L/month — enough to be primary income but wants to scale higher
  • Tools: EK Affiliaters for auto-forward, Magic Tool for manual picks, Excel for tracking earnings
  • Pain points: Commission confirmation delays, limited merchant catalog, wants analytics but doesn't have any, feels stuck at current earnings level
  • Personality: Data-driven, responds to numbers, wants to grow but needs guidance, skeptical of promises but open to testing
  • Approach: Lead with earnings comparison data. Show specific merchants they're missing. Offer growth coaching as a differentiator. The 30-day parallel trial resonates strongly with this persona.
Persona B: "The Homemaker Hustler" (Tier 3-4)
  • Demographics: Female, 28-45 years old, homemaker looking for income while managing household, based in metro or tier-1 city
  • Channel: 1 channel with 15K-100K subscribers, focused on fashion, beauty, home & kitchen deals
  • Daily routine: Posts between household chores, most active between 10 AM-1 PM and 3-5 PM, posts 10-20 deals per day
  • Earnings: ₹15K-80K/month — significant supplemental income, takes pride in financial independence
  • Tools: Basic EK Affiliaters setup, may not be using all features, prefers simplicity
  • Pain points: Limited time, needs easy-to-use tools, wants higher earnings per deal rather than more deals, frustrated by technical complexity
  • Personality: Community-oriented, values relationships, appreciates personal attention, word-of-mouth is powerful — will recommend to friends if happy
  • Approach: Lead with simplicity and personal support. Offer white-glove setup. Emphasize the community aspect. The mentorship and training program resonates strongly. Keep communication warm and supportive, not salesy.
Persona C: "The Student Side-Hustler" (Tier 4-5)
  • Demographics: Male/Female, 18-24 years old, college student, tech-savvy, looking for income to support education or lifestyle expenses
  • Channel: 1 channel with 2K-30K subscribers, often focused on electronics, mobile deals, or a niche (gaming, fashion subcategory)
  • Daily routine: Posts between classes, most active evenings and weekends, posts 5-15 deals per day
  • Earnings: ₹3K-20K/month — meaningful pocket money, dreams of making it a full-time career
  • Tools: May be using free tools or basic EK setup, quick to adopt new technology
  • Pain points: Small subscriber base, low earnings, needs growth guidance, limited budget for promotions
  • Personality: Eager, ambitious, quick learner, responsive to aspirational messaging (success stories of other students who scaled), active on social media
  • Approach: Lead with the "Born on GrabCash" program. Share success stories of students who built 100K+ channels. Emphasize free training, mentorship, and the ₹1,000 welcome bonus. They respond well to milestone rewards (bonuses at 5K, 10K, 25K subs).
Persona D: "The Deal Empire Builder" (Tier 1-2)
  • Demographics: Male, 28-40, former tech/marketing professional, runs it as a serious business with team
  • Channel: 3-8 channels with 500K-2M+ combined subscribers, covers all major categories, may also run YouTube/website
  • Daily routine: Manages team, reviews analytics, negotiates with networks, 100+ posts/day across channels via bots
  • Earnings: ₹5L-15L+/month, possibly ₹1Cr+ annually — this is a full business operation
  • Tools: Custom bot setups, multiple affiliate platforms (EK + CueLinks + direct), possibly custom link shortener
  • Pain points: Wants better merchant rates (negotiates hard), needs reliable high-volume infrastructure, concerned about platform dependency, wants a seat at the table
  • Personality: Business-minded, negotiates everything, values partnership over vendor relationship, influenced by strategic vision not tactical offers
  • Approach: PARTNERSHIP framing only. Never pitch; propose. Offer advisory board seat, revenue guarantees, co-development of tools. They need to feel like equals, not recruits. Direct access to GrabCash leadership is essential.
