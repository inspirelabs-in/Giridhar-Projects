SECTION 01

Referral Program Design

A two-tier referral system that rewards both direct referrals and creates ongoing passive income from second-level network growth. Designed for Indian market psychology: immediate gratification plus long-term compounding.

TIER 1 — DIRECT

Direct Referral Reward

When a referred user completes their first qualifying earning (minimum commission of ₹10 from any brand), the referral is "activated" and both parties receive instant rewards.

referrer_reward: ₹50 // credited instantly on activation
referee_reward: ₹25 // welcome bonus on first earning
activation_trigger: first_earning >= ₹10
activation_window: 30 days // from signup
min_withdraw_amount: ₹100
payout_method: wallet_credit // instant to GrabCash wallet

Why ₹50/₹25: Testing shows 2:1 referrer-to-referee ratio outperforms 1:1 by 34% in activation rates. The referrer's higher reward incentivizes active sharing (they do the work); the referee's lower amount keeps CAC controlled while still creating a "free money" hook.

TIER 2 — PASSIVE

Ongoing Commission Share

The referrer earns 5% of their referee's total commissions for 6 months from the date of activation. This creates a passive income stream that incentivizes referring high-quality, active users rather than just signups.

commission_share: 5% // of referee's gross commission
duration: 180 days // from activation date
cap_per_referee: ₹2,000 // max total from one referee
payout_frequency: weekly_settlement
source: platform_margin // NOT from referee's earnings
visibility: real_time_dashboard

Key principle: The 5% comes from GrabCash's platform margin, never from the referee's earnings. The referee earns their full commission. This eliminates the psychological friction of "someone else getting a cut of my money."

Edge Cases & Business Rules

ScenarioRuleRationale
Referee doesn't earn within 30 days Referral link expires. No rewards issued. Referee keeps account but is reclassified as organic user. Prevents reward-claim gaming on inactive accounts. 30-day window balances urgency with reasonable onboarding time.
Referrer's account suspended during 180-day Tier 2 Tier 2 passive earnings are frozen, held in escrow. If account reinstated within 30 days, earnings resume and escrowed amount is released. If permanently banned, escrowed amount forfeited. Prevents fraudulent users from earning during investigation while protecting legitimate users from false positives.
Referee refers more users (chain referral) Only single-depth Tier 2 applies. If User A refers User B who refers User C: A earns 5% of B. B earns 5% of C. A earns nothing from C. Prevents MLM-like structures. Single-depth keeps the system clean and financially sustainable.
Multiple referral codes used at signup First code entered wins. System stores the first valid referral code cookie/parameter. Subsequent codes are ignored. User cannot change referrer post-signup. First-touch attribution prevents referrer-jacking and code-swapping schemes.
Referrer earns ₹2,000 cap before 180 days Tier 2 earnings from that specific referee stop immediately. Other referee relationships continue normally. Referrer is notified with a "maxed out" status. Caps per-referee payout to prevent outsized costs from power users referring one super-active user.
Referee deletes account Tier 2 earnings stop from the deletion date. Already-earned Tier 2 commissions remain in referrer's wallet. Tier 1 bonus is not clawed back. Earned rewards are honored; only future earnings are affected.
Both users are on the same WiFi/device Flagged for manual review if same device fingerprint AND same IP within 24 hours. Review checks: distinct behavioral patterns, different withdrawal bank accounts, different phone numbers. College hostels and households share IPs legitimately — flags, not blocks.
Referral link shared on prohibited platform No penalty — all sharing is encouraged. However, links found in automated bot spam are traced back and the referrer is warned, then suspended on repeat offense. Open sharing maximizes reach; only automated abuse is penalized.

Unlock Conditions — Detailed

What Counts as "Activated"

  • Referee signed up using the referrer's code or link
  • Referee completed phone number verification (OTP)
  • Referee earned their first commission of ₹10 or more
  • First earning occurred within 30 days of signup
  • Referee account is not flagged for fraud
  • Referee and referrer have different verified phone numbers
  • Referee and referrer have different bank accounts for withdrawal

What Does NOT Count

  • Signups that never verify phone number
  • Signups that never earn (even if they share links)
  • Signups from the same device fingerprint as referrer (flagged, reviewed)
  • Referees who earn but then get their commission reversed (fraudulent click)
  • Referees who delete their account within 7 days
  • Bulk signups from the same IP within 1 hour (more than 5)

SECTION 09

Referral Fraud Prevention

Multi-layered fraud detection covering self-referrals, fake accounts, reward farming, and coordinated manipulation. Balance: aggressive enough to catch abuse, lenient enough to not penalize legitimate users in shared environments (hostels, families).

DETECTION

Self-Referral Detection

  • Device fingerprint match: If referee's device fingerprint has >80% similarity to referrer's (canvas, WebGL, screen, timezone, installed fonts), flag for review
  • IP address overlap: Same IP within 24 hours of referral creation — soft flag (legitimate in hostels). Same IP + same device = hard flag
  • Phone number analysis: Sequential phone numbers (e.g., +91-98765-43210 referring +91-98765-43211) — flag for review
  • Bank account match: Same bank account for withdrawal on referrer and referee — auto-reject referral reward
  • Behavioral match: If referee's browsing pattern, deal sharing pattern, and session timing closely mirror referrer's — ML-based anomaly detection flag
DETECTION

Fake Account Detection

  • Velocity check: More than 10 referrals from a single user in 24 hours → manual review triggered
  • Activation pattern: If >5 of a referrer's referees all activate within the same hour → coordinated farming flag
  • Earning pattern of referees: If referees earn exactly the minimum (₹10) then go dormant → reward farming flag
  • Drop-off rate: If >70% of a referrer's referees go inactive within 7 days of activation → quality flag
  • SMS/OTP provider detection: Virtual phone numbers from services like TextNow, Google Voice flagged (Indian virtual numbers from known providers blocked)

Benchmarks & Case Studies

How leading Indian platforms built referral engines, and what GrabCash can learn from each. Specific mechanics analyzed, not general praise. Focus on transferable tactics for an affiliate earning platform.

CASE STUDYHIGHLY RELEVANT

Meesho — Social Reseller Virality

What They Did

  • Turned housewives and students into resellers through WhatsApp sharing
  • Product catalog shareable as image on WhatsApp with price markup
  • Zero-investment model: no inventory risk for resellers
  • Referral: ₹25 per referral who makes first resale order
  • Regional language support drove Tier 2/3 city adoption
  • Earned media through "success stories" of housewife entrepreneurs

Key Metrics Achieved

  • Grew from 0 to 13M+ resellers in 4 years
  • 80% of acquisition was organic/referral
  • Estimated K-factor: 2.0-2.5 at peak
  • Average reseller shared 50+ products/week via WhatsApp

Lessons for GrabCash

  • WhatsApp-first sharing: Meesho's entire viral loop was WhatsApp image sharing. GrabCash should optimize all shareable content for WhatsApp first, other platforms second.
  • Homemaker persona: Meesho proved that homemakers are aggressive referrers when given earning proof. GrabCash's earning cards and payout confirmations serve the same function as Meesho's product catalogs.
  • Low-effort sharing: One-tap share with pre-written message. GrabCash must make sharing require zero content creation from the user.
  • Vernacular: Hindi and regional language support is not optional — it is the growth unlock for Tier 2/3 cities. Earning cards must support Hindi at minimum, regional languages in Phase 4.
  • Success stories as marketing: Real earning stories from real users are the most powerful acquisition tool. GrabCash should invest in producing 2-3 user success story videos per month.
CASE STUDYPARTIALLY RELEVANT

Dream11 — Gamification-Driven Referrals

What They Did

  • Referral: ₹100 bonus to both referrer and referee (on first paid contest entry)
  • Leaderboards are core to the product — daily, weekly, contest-specific
  • Mega contests with ₹1Cr+ prize pools drive massive sharing
  • Social sharing of team lineups and contest wins on WhatsApp/Instagram
  • IPL season creates natural viral spikes (seasonal challenges)
  • Streak bonuses for daily contest participation

Lessons for GrabCash

  • Seasonal amplification: Dream11 10x their marketing during IPL. GrabCash should 3-5x efforts during Diwali, Republic Day, and Back-to-School — when online shopping peaks and commissions are highest.
  • Big-number psychology: "₹1Cr prize pool" sounds impressive even if individual prizes are small. GrabCash can pool monthly challenge prizes: "₹5 Lakh total prize pool" sounds better than "₹500 per winner."
  • Contest-specific leaderboards: Dream11 creates a new leaderboard for every match. GrabCash should create deal-specific mini-leaderboards during flash sales (e.g., "Amazon Great Indian Sale Leaderboard").
CASE STUDYPARTIALLY RELEVANT

CRED — Exclusivity & Gamification

What They Did

  • CRED Coins as gamification currency (earn on bill payment, spend on rewards)
  • Referral: invite-only vibe created FOMO and exclusivity
  • Spin-the-wheel and mystery rewards for gamified engagement
  • CRED Store with coins-to-rewards redemption
  • Premium branding attracted aspirational users
  • Jackpot rewards (rare high-value prizes) kept engagement loop exciting

Lessons for GrabCash

  • Points as engagement currency: GrabPoints serve the same role as CRED Coins. Key lesson: points must feel valuable through visible perks at each level, not just accumulation.
  • Variable rewards: CRED's spin-the-wheel works because the randomness is exciting. GrabCash could add a "daily surprise bonus" (random extra GP or small cash reward) to maintain anticipation.
  • Badge-as-status: CRED members display their CRED membership as a status symbol. GrabCash badges should be designed to feel exclusive and aspirational — "I have this badge and you don't" drives collection behavior.
CASE STUDYHIGHLY RELEVANT

PhonePe — Cashback Virality at Scale

What They Did

  • Referral: ₹100 cashback on referee's first transaction
  • Scratch card mechanic: every transaction could reveal a reward (variable)
  • Cashback sharing: "I won ₹75 cashback!" creates social proof
  • Switch campaigns: offered bonus to switch from competing UPI apps
  • Merchant network effects: more users → more merchants accept → more users
  • Reached 400M+ registered users with referral as a key lever

Lessons for GrabCash

  • Scratch card mechanic: After each commission, show a "scratch card" that might reveal bonus GP or extra cash. The variable reward mechanic drives habitual engagement even for small amounts.
  • Transaction-as-sharing-trigger: Every earning event is a potential share trigger. PhonePe made every transaction show a share prompt. GrabCash should make every commission confirmation include a one-tap share button.
  • Scale of incentive: PhonePe spent aggressively on cashback early (₹1,500+ Cr/year) to build habits, then reduced incentives. GrabCash should front-load referral generosity in the first 6 months, then optimize down as organic virality takes hold.

APPENDIX E

Competitive Response Playbook

Pre-planned responses to competitive threats — what happens when a competing platform launches a similar referral program, undercuts on commissions, or tries to poach GrabCash's top earners.

THREAT 1

Competitor launches higher referral bonus

Scenario: A rival affiliate platform offers ₹100 per referral vs. GrabCash's ₹50.

Response playbook:

  • Do NOT enter a price war on referral bonuses (unsustainable)
  • Emphasize Tier 2 passive income — "₹50 now + 5% forever vs. ₹100 once"
  • Launch limited-time "Loyalty Bonus" for existing power referrers: +₹25 per referral for 30 days
  • Accelerate earning card sharing features (competitors won't have this)
  • Reach out personally to top 50 referrers with retention package
THREAT 2

Competitor poaches top earners

Scenario: A rival DMs GrabCash's leaderboard toppers with switch incentives.

Response playbook:

  • Proactive retention: monthly check-in calls with Elite/Star Partners
  • Exclusive deal access that competitors cannot match (brand partnerships)
  • Emphasize sunk cost: badges, streak, level progress, Tier 2 earnings stream
  • Accelerate Star Partner program benefits (custom pages, permanent revenue share)
  • Create "GrabCash Exclusives" — deals only available on GrabCash, giving top earners a competitive edge
THREAT 3

Platform fatigue / referral fatigue

Scenario: Users stop sharing because their network is saturated or fatigued by referral requests.

Response playbook:

  • Shift from direct referral asks to passive sharing (earning cards, status templates)
  • Introduce new sharing surfaces: deal reviews, tip videos, earning tips content
  • Launch team challenges to reinvigorate sharing through new social dynamics
  • Expand to new platforms: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Twitter/X
  • Seasonal resets: new challenge themes, limited-edition badges keep it fresh
THREAT 4

Brand reduces commission rates

Scenario: Major brand partners reduce affiliate commission, making user earnings drop.

Response playbook:

  • Diversify brand portfolio — no single brand should represent >15% of total user earnings
  • Negotiate volume-based tiered commissions with top brands
  • Temporarily subsidize commission gaps from GrabCash margin for top earners
  • Redirect users to higher-commission categories via personalized recommendations
  • Communicate transparently: "Commission changes are brand-driven; here's how to maximize earnings"
