When NEVER to Prompt

Review Prompt Blacklist - NEVER Trigger During These Events

  • After an error or crash recovery
  • During or after a cashback tracking failure
  • After a payment delay beyond 48 hours
  • After a cashback claim is denied or disputed
  • During the first session (user has no experience yet)
  • After a customer support complaint
  • When the user is browsing without completing a transaction
  • After force-closing and reopening the app
  • More than once per 30 days (frequency cap)
  • If the user has already reviewed the app

The First 90 Days Determine Everything

App Store algorithms heavily weight the first 90 days of an app's life. Install velocity, rating trajectory, engagement signals, and update frequency during this window establish the app's long-term ranking ceiling. This plan is sequenced to maximize every ranking signal in the critical launch window.

  • Write and optimize short description (5 variations ready for testing)
  • Write full description (4,000 chars, keyword-optimized)
