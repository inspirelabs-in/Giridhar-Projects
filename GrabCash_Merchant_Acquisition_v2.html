4-Phase Merchant Conversion Strategy

Phase 1: Auto-Enroll via Existing Affiliate APIs (Days 1-15)
TARGET: 500+ MERCHANTS LIVE IN 15 DAYS

Many GrabOn merchants already have affiliate programs via networks (Admitad, vCommission, Impact). GrabCash can register as a sub-affiliate and begin tracking immediately without any merchant contact.

  • Amazon Associates: Register GrabCash as an affiliate. Tag generation is instant. All Amazon products live on Day 1.
  • Flipkart Affiliate: Apply for Flipkart affiliate access. Approval typically within 48 hours. Full catalog available.
  • Admitad Merchants: Join Admitad as publisher. 2,000+ advertisers available. Bulk approval for established publishers.
  • vCommission Merchants: Register on vCommission. Request access to top 200 campaigns. Most approve within 24-72 hours.
  • Impact Merchants: Apply to Impact.com programs. Global brands with India operations.
Key Insight: GrabOn's existing network credentials (volume history, compliance record) means GrabCash gets fast-tracked on network approvals. Leverage GrabOn's publisher ID where possible.
Phase 2: Upgrade GrabOn Merchants to GrabCash Tracking (Days 15-45)
TARGET: 2,000+ MERCHANTS MIGRATED

Contact GrabOn merchant partners directly. Pitch GrabCash as an additional distribution channel for their existing coupon deals.

  • Use GrabOn BD team's existing contacts — warm introductions, not cold outreach
  • Offer merchants dual-listing: coupons on GrabOn + cashback on GrabCash
  • Highlight incremental traffic: GrabCash's Telegram/WhatsApp audience is complementary, not cannibalistic
  • Propose a 90-day pilot with zero additional cost to merchants
  • Provide real-time dashboard showing GrabCash-attributed conversions
Phase 3: Negotiate Enhanced Rates for High-Volume Categories (Days 30-60)
TARGET: 15-25% RATE IMPROVEMENT ON TOP 100 MERCHANTS

Once GrabCash demonstrates volume, negotiate improved commission rates by leveraging combined GrabOn + GrabCash traffic.

  • Present combined traffic data: "GrabOn sends X clicks/month, GrabCash adds Y new users from social channels"
  • Negotiate volume tiers: base rate + bonus at 500/1000/5000 conversions per month
  • Request category exclusivity windows during major sales (Republic Day, Diwali, etc.)
  • Propose co-funded cashback boosts: merchant funds 50%, GrabCash funds 50%
Phase 4: Exclusive GrabCash-Only Deals (Days 45-90)
TARGET: 50+ EXCLUSIVE MERCHANT DEALS

Create deals that are only available through GrabCash, providing competitive differentiation.

  • Extra cashback on specific categories exclusive to GrabCash users
  • First-access to sale launches (GrabCash users get 2-hour early access)
  • Merchant-branded GrabCash campaigns (e.g., "Nykaa x GrabCash Beauty Week")
  • Bundle deals: cashback + coupon stacking only via GrabCash

  2. Network with highest commission for the specific category/product
  3. Network with best conversion tracking (real-time vs delayed reporting)

Link Tracking Architecture

Tracking Flow

Every affiliate link follows this tracking chain:

  1. User clicks GrabCash link → GrabCash tracking pixel fires, records click_id, affiliate_id, merchant_id, timestamp, user_agent, IP
  2. 302 redirect to merchant → Appends network tracking parameters (sub_id, aff_id)
  3. User completes purchase → Merchant/network postback fires to GrabCash server
  4. Conversion recorded → Match click_id with conversion, calculate commission waterfall
  5. Cashback credited → After merchant validation period (7-60 days), credit user wallet

TDS Compliance (Section 194H)

TDS on Commission Payments

Under Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, GrabCash must deduct TDS on commission payments to merchants/affiliates:

  • Rate: 5% TDS on commission/brokerage payments (10% if PAN not provided)
  • Threshold: TDS applicable when total commission exceeds ₹15,000 per financial year per payee
  • Filing: Quarterly TDS returns (Form 26Q), annual TDS certificate (Form 16A)
  • Timeline: TDS to be deposited by 7th of following month (April deadline: 30th April)
  • Automation: TDS calculation to be built into payout system; auto-generate Form 16A for merchants

Investigation Process

Fraud Investigation Workflow
1
Detection & Alert

Automated system flags anomalous patterns. Alert sent to Fraud Review Team within 1 hour of detection.

2
Initial Assessment (24 hours)

Fraud analyst reviews flagged transactions. Classifies as: False Positive, Suspicious, or Confirmed Fraud. Account placed on hold if Suspicious/Confirmed.

3
Deep Investigation (72 hours)

Full audit of transaction history, IP logs, device fingerprints, click patterns. Contact affected merchant for corroboration.

4
Resolution & Action (48 hours)

Determination made. If fraud confirmed: commission clawback, account ban, merchant notification. If false positive: account restored, apology communication.

5
Post-Incident Review

Update detection algorithms. Document case for training. Quarterly fraud report to leadership and key merchants.
