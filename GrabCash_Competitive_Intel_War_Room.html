Step 4: Trigger appropriate response: Type A → Welcome + optimize onboarding Type B → Increase incentives to complete switch Type C → Retention team engagement within 2 hours

Ethical Boundaries

Fair Game (Permitted)

  • Monitoring publicly available information (websites, apps, social media)
  • Using competitor products as a regular user to test features
  • Networking at industry events and conferences
  • Reading public financial filings and press releases
  • Tracking app store updates and changelogs
  • Monitoring job postings for hiring signals
  • Analyzing publicly shared Telegram channel data
  • Building relationships with admins currently on competitor platforms

Off Limits (Prohibited)

  • Hacking, unauthorized access to competitor systems
  • Impersonating competitor employees
  • Bribing competitor employees for proprietary information
  • Stealing trade secrets or proprietary code
  • Creating fake accounts to access restricted competitor data
  • Spreading misinformation about competitors
  • Violating terms of service of competitor platforms
  • Intercepting private communications
