Important Tax Notices

  • Form 26AS: Creators should verify TDS deductions on Form 26AS to claim credit while filing ITR.

CashKaro's Creator Approach

CashKaro has experimented with a "CashKaro Stars" concept but has not formalized it into a structured program. Their focus remains on end-user cashback rather than creator-driven distribution. CashKaro's browser extension and app-first approach means they capture users who are already shopping, rather than using creators to drive discovery and intent. This is a fundamentally different model from what GrabCash should build.

  • Strength: Strong brand recognition (funded by Kalaari Capital, backed by Ratan Tata)
  • Weakness: No formal creator program, no content tools, no creator dashboard
