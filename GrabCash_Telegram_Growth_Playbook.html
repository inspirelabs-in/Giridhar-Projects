🔍 Deal Curation Methodology

Every deal passes through a 5-point quality filter before posting. This ensures consistent value and builds subscriber trust.

Quality Filter Checklist

  1. Discount Depth: Minimum 40% off MRP or historically lowest price. Verify using price trackers (PriceHistory, Keepa for Amazon).
  2. Brand Trust Score: Only products from brands with 3.5+ star average ratings. No unbranded or unknown sellers.
  3. Stock Verification: Confirm product is in stock before posting. Dead links outperform trust faster than anything.
  4. Commission Viability: Minimum ₹15 estimated commission per conversion. Below this, the post isn't worth the subscriber attention cost.
  5. Relevance Score: Does this deal match current seasonal demand? Winter jackets in June = skip. Back-to-school electronics in July = post.
