Brand Deduplication Rules

When a brand is available on multiple networks, apply these priority rules:

  • Rule 1 — Direct Integration First: If GrabCash has a direct brand partnership, always use direct tracking (higher commission, better data)
  • Rule 2 — Highest Commission Network: Among networks, route traffic to the one offering highest effective commission
  • Rule 3 — Best Tracking Reliability: If commission rates are similar (within 0.5%), prefer the network with better tracking/attribution
  • Rule 4 — Fastest Payout: As tiebreaker, prefer networks with faster payout cycles
  • Rule 5 — Manual Override: BD team can manually assign a brand to a specific network for strategic reasons

Brand Churn Prevention Playbook

When a brand shows signs of churn (health score dropping, no deal refreshes, declining volume), activate this intervention sequence:

Day 1 — Early Warning Detected

Health Score Alert Triggered

System detects health score dropping below 60. AM receives automated alert with specific declining metrics and recommended intervention talking points.

Day 1-2 — Personal Outreach

AM Calls Brand Contact

AM places a personal call (not email) to the brand contact. Goal: understand what's changed. Common reasons: budget cuts, team changes, tracking issues, seasonal downturn, competitive offer. Document findings in CRM.

Day 3-5 — Value Reinforcement

Custom Performance Review Sent

AM sends a personalized performance review highlighting: total revenue generated to date, CPA comparison vs other channels, top-performing deal highlights, recommendations for improvement. Include specific next steps.

Day 7 — Incentive Offer

Retention Incentive Deployed

If standard outreach hasn't re-engaged the brand, offer a time-limited incentive: 30-day commission rate reduction (GrabCash absorbs the margin), free premium placement for 2 weeks, dedicated sharer campaign at no extra cost, or a co-marketing feature in GrabOn's newsletter.

Day 14 — Executive Escalation

Head of Partnerships Intervenes

If health score is still declining, Head of Partnerships makes a personal call to the brand's senior decision-maker. Different conversation: strategic partnership value, roadmap preview, custom terms negotiation, executive commitment.

Day 21+ — Graceful Wind-Down or Win-Back

Final Decision Point

If brand decides to leave: execute graceful wind-down (disable deals, final payout, exit survey). Add to quarterly win-back campaign list. If brand stays: document intervention success for team learning. Update playbook with new insights.
