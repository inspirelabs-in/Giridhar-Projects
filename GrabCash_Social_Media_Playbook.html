2h. Growth Tactics

Reels Algorithm Optimization

  • Hook in 0.5 seconds: First frame must have text overlay or dramatic visual. No slow intros.
  • Optimal length: 7-15 seconds for virality, 30-60 seconds for depth. Avoid 15-30 second dead zone.
  • Loop potential: Design endings that connect to beginnings for automatic replay (counts as multiple views).
  • Trending audio: Use trending sounds within the first 48 hours of them trending. Check Reels tab daily for new trending audio.
  • Caption hooks: First line of caption must create curiosity. "The trick nobody talks about..." or "Save this for your next Amazon order..."
  • Share triggers: Content people share via DM to friends ranks highest. Create "tag someone who needs this" moments.
  • Post 2 Reels/day minimum: The algorithm rewards volume during growth phase. Quality must stay high.
  • Engage for 30 minutes post-upload: Reply to every comment, answer DMs, view stories. Signals to the algorithm that the post is active.

Collaboration Strategy

Collab Reels

Partner with finance creators, student influencers, and deal-sharing accounts for joint Reels. Both accounts get the reach. Target 4 collab Reels per month.

  • Find creators using hashtag research in the earning/finance niche
  • Offer free premium access or guaranteed minimum earnings for the collaboration
  • Co-create content: one person demonstrates earning, the other reacts
  • Use Instagram's native Collab feature (post appears on both profiles)
Engagement Pod (Internal Team)

Create a private group of 15-20 people (team + power users) who engage with every post within the first 15 minutes of posting.

  • Like, comment (meaningful, 4+ words), save, and share every post
  • Comment engagement in the first 15 minutes is the strongest ranking signal
  • Rotate comment styles to avoid detection (questions, compliments, tags)
  • Never use bots or automation; Instagram penalizes artificial patterns

Testing Process

  1. Hypothesis: Clearly state what you believe and why. Example: "Reels starting with a question hook will have 20% higher completion rate than statement hooks because questions create a knowledge gap."
  2. Isolate Variable: Change only ONE thing. Keep everything else identical (same topic, same length, same time, same day of week).
  3. Minimum Sample: Wait until each variant has at least 1000 views (for Reels) or 500 impressions (for ads) before drawing conclusions.
  4. Statistical Significance: A 5% difference on 1000 views is noise. Look for 15%+ differences or test longer.
  5. Document Results: Record in shared Notion database: test name, hypothesis, variants, metrics, winner, learnings. Build institutional knowledge.
  6. Implement Winner: Roll the winning variant into the standard content playbook. Test the next variable.

New Platform Launch Checklist

Use this checklist when expanding GrabCash's presence to a new social platform or launching a new channel.

  1. Account Setup: Create account with official brand name, upload logo, complete all profile fields, verify if possible.
  2. Bio/Description: Write platform-specific bio with clear value proposition, CTA, and link to grabcash.in.
  3. Visual Assets: Design platform-specific banner, profile image, and content templates matching brand guidelines.
  4. Content Backlog: Prepare at least 2 weeks of content before going live. Never launch with an empty account.
  5. Cross-Promotion Plan: Announce the new presence on all existing channels. Run a "follow us on [platform]" campaign.
  6. Content Pillars: Define which content pillars apply to this platform and in what ratio.
  7. Posting Schedule: Research optimal posting times for the platform's Indian audience. Set a sustainable cadence.
  8. Engagement Protocol: Define response time SLAs, comment management rules, and escalation procedures for this platform.
  9. Analytics Setup: Add the platform to the tracking dashboard. Define KPIs and initial targets for Month 1.
  10. Team Assignment: Assign a specific team member as the owner/DRI (directly responsible individual) for this platform.
  11. 30-Day Review: Schedule a review at day 30 to evaluate whether the platform is worth continued investment.

Content Quality Checklist (Pre-Publish)

Run every piece of content through this checklist before hitting publish.

Visual Quality

  • Image/video is high resolution (not pixelated or blurry)
  • Text is readable on mobile (test on phone screen)
  • Brand colors are consistent (GrabCash green, dark theme)
  • No spelling errors in on-screen text
  • Logo watermark present (if applicable)
  • Subtitles/captions added for video content
  • Aspect ratio correct for the platform
  • No competitor branding accidentally visible

Content Quality

  • Hook is strong and appears in first 1.5 seconds (video) or first line (text)
  • Value is clear: what does the viewer learn, gain, or feel?
  • No income guarantees or misleading claims
  • CTA is present and clear
  • Earnings screenshots have personal info blurred
  • Pricing information is current and accurate
  • Hashtags are included (25-30 for IG, relevant for others)
  • UTM-tagged link is in the appropriate place (bio, description, etc.)

Brand Safety

  • No competitor names mentioned in a negative context
  • Compliant with ASCI advertising guidelines
  • Influencer content includes #Ad or #Sponsored tag
  • No copyrighted music used without license
  • User testimonials have consent/permission
  • Brand voice matches the platform-specific tone guidelines
  • Content reviewed by at least one other team member
  • Approved by content lead (for sensitive topics only)
