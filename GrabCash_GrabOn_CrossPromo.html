The Conversion Thesis

GrabOn users are already deal-conscious shoppers who actively seek savings. The mental leap from "save money while shopping" to "earn money by sharing deals" is minimal. These users already understand affiliate links, coupon mechanics, and brand deals — they just don't realize they can monetize this behavior.

Key Insight

Every GrabOn user who copies a coupon code and shares it with a friend is already doing what GrabCash users do — minus the earning. We just need to formalize the earning mechanism.

💰

Value Exchange

GrabOn gives users savings (average ₹200-500 per transaction). GrabCash offers a fundamentally different proposition: recurring income. The psychology shifts from "I saved ₹300" to "I earned ₹300." This is a powerful upgrade in perceived value.

Behavioral Economics

Earning ₹100 triggers a stronger positive response than saving ₹100 (gain framing vs. loss prevention). GrabCash leverages this asymmetry.

Risk Analysis & Mitigation

⚠ Cannibalization Concern

Risk: GrabOn users switch to GrabCash for their own purchases, reducing GrabOn's affiliate revenue.

Mitigation Strategy

  • Position GrabCash as "share with others" platform, not "save for yourself"
  • Maintain GrabOn for personal savings, GrabCash for earning
  • Track cannibalization metrics weekly — if GrabOn revenue drops >3%, slow rollout
  • Net revenue analysis: even if 10% cannibalization, net positive with GrabCash growth

Medium Risk • Highly Mitigatable

⚠ User Experience Dilution

Risk: Too many GrabCash CTAs degrade GrabOn user experience, reducing coupon engagement.

Mitigation Strategy

  • Strict CTA density rules: max 2 GrabCash touchpoints per page
  • A/B test every placement before full rollout
  • Monitor GrabOn bounce rate and session duration weekly
  • Quick kill-switch for any placement that hurts metrics
  • Use contextual, helpful CTAs (not intrusive ads)

Medium Risk • Fully Controllable

⚠ Brand Partner Pushback

Risk: Brands worry that GrabCash earners will spam their deals, creating brand perception issues.

Mitigation Strategy

  • Present as "earn ecosystem" that drives MORE sales for brands
  • Offer brands analytics on GrabCash-driven conversions
  • Content guidelines for earners to maintain brand quality
  • Pilot with 10 brand-friendly merchants first

Low-Medium Risk

⚠ Internal Resource Conflict

Risk: GrabOn team resists allocating resources to GrabCash promotion, viewing it as a distraction.

Mitigation Strategy

  • Executive mandate from InspireLabs leadership
  • Shared KPIs: GrabOn team incentivized on GrabCash conversions
  • Dedicated cross-promo resource (1 person) to minimize GrabOn team burden
  • Show early wins to build internal momentum
